Child Safety Standards

Last updated July 23, 2026

Narrows has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). This policy describes our standards, prevention practices, and how to report a concern.

1. Age requirement

Users must be at least 13 years old to create a Narrows account. We do not knowingly allow accounts for anyone under 13, and we remove any account we identify as belonging to a user under 13.

2. Zero tolerance for CSAE

Narrows strictly prohibits any content or behavior that sexualizes, exploits, or endangers minors, including but not limited to child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, or solicitation of minors. Any account found to violate this policy is permanently removed and banned, regardless of context or intent.

3. Reporting a concern

Any user can report a post, comment, or account directly in the app using the "•••" menu or press-and-hold report action. You can also email us directly at hello@narrows.site with the word "urgent" in the subject line for anything involving a child's safety.

4. Our response

Reports involving suspected CSAE are treated as our highest priority and are reviewed as soon as they are received, typically within 24 hours. Confirmed violations result in immediate content removal and permanent account termination. As a small independent developer, Narrows does not yet operate an automated CSAM-detection pipeline or a formal NCMEC reporting integration; all reports are reviewed manually by the developer. Where required by law, we cooperate with law enforcement and report confirmed CSAM to the appropriate authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), on a case-by-case basis.

5. Point of contact

Our designated point of contact for child safety and CSAM compliance questions is hello@narrows.site.
