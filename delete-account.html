Delete Your Account

Last updated July 23, 2026

You can request deletion of your Narrows account and the personal data associated with it at any time. There are two ways to do this.

Option 1 — Delete in the app

  1. Open the Narrows app and go to your Profile tab.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear) icon.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account is deactivated immediately and permanently removed shortly after.

Option 2 — Request by email

Email us from the address you used to sign up and we will delete your account for you.

Email hello@narrows.site

Please include the email address associated with your Narrows account so we can locate it. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days of your request.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your profile (name, username, photo, and bio).
  • Your posts and comments.
  • Your catch pins and any location data associated with them.
  • Your account credentials and sign-in identifiers.

What may be retained

We may retain a limited amount of information where required by law, to resolve disputes, prevent fraud and abuse, or enforce our Terms of Service — for example, records related to a reported safety or abuse issue. Any retained data is kept only as long as necessary and is then deleted.

Questions

If you have any questions about deleting your account or your data, email hello@narrows.site or see our Privacy Policy.
