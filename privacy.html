Privacy Policy

Last updated July 20, 2026

Narrows respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through narrows.site and how we use it.

Information we collect

If you apply for Android early access, we collect:

  • Your email address

We also use Google Analytics to understand site traffic — see "Analytics" below. We don't collect any other personal information through this website.

How we use your information

  • To email you an Android tester invite once a spot opens up
  • To send occasional updates about the app

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent, or share your email address with third parties for marketing purposes
  • We don't use your email for anything beyond communicating about Narrows

Your choices

Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link. You can opt out at any time, and we'll remove you from our list.

Data storage

Emails submitted through this site are stored with our email service provider, Loops, which handles sending on our behalf. You can read more about how Loops handles data at loops.so/privacy.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use narrows.site — pages viewed, general location (country/city level), device type, and which links get clicked (like our App Store and Android tester buttons). This data is aggregated and isn't used to identify you individually. You can read about Google's data practices at policies.google.com/privacy, and opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at hello@narrows.site.
